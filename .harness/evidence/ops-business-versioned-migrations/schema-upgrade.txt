business schema was accepted by the idempotent CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS
statements without data changes. Duplicate active scope and task/host writes
were rejected with MySQL error 1062. A full legacy AutoMigrate index-shape
conversion was not run; existing pre-migration tables with legacy unique index
names remain a residual upgrade risk and require a follow-up conditional ALTER.
